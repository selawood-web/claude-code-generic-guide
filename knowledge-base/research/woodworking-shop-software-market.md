## Findings

### Market size & structure (stable)
- ~6,000 kitchen/bath/cabinet manufacturing companies in the US; segment revenue ~$9B
  ([Woodworking Network](https://woodworkingnetwork.com/resources/market-data/How-Many-Cabinet-Companies-Are-There-in-the-US-229407561.html)).
- Custom woodwork manufacturing market ~$8.9B; craftsman-driven, project-based,
  local/regional shops dominate ([Kentley Insights](https://www.kentleyinsights.com/custom-woodwork-manufacturing-market-report/)).
- Beyond registered manufacturers there is a long tail of solo/semi-pro furniture makers
  (size unquantified in this research pass).

### The gap (stable)
- Explicitly documented: small shops want "optimize a cut, send a branded quote, know
  what materials I own and what each job cost" — currently served by spreadsheets +
  QuickBooks or overkill CAM suites ([EZNESTING](https://eznesting.com/blog/cabinet-shop-software)).
- Pricing work is the universally admitted #1 business pain
  ([The Wood Whisperer](https://thewoodwhisperer.com/articles/pricing-your-work/)).
- Job costing (quoted vs. actual margin) is the wedge JoineryCore markets on.

### AI landscape (volatile)
- Consumer AI apps (Wood AI, WoodCraft AI, Woodify) do species ID and photo→plan for
  hobbyists; none connect plans to a shop's real costs, rates, or quoting.
- 2026 industry commentary frames AI as concept generation + nesting optimization +
  client presentation ([Toolstoday](https://toolstoday.com/learn/incorporating-ai-into-the-woodworking-design-process)).
