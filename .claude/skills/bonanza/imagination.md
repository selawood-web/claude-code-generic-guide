# Imagination — Tools, Lens Packs, the Generator's Contract

Given to generators only (see `SKILL.md`, Step 3). A judge never reads this file.

The tools are ways of seeing and moving, not ready answers. **A tool opens; it does
not answer.** None of them says what to find, only how to look. That is deliberate:
a tool that arrives with an example attached pulls every idea toward the example.
Do not add examples to this file.

## Perception tools — how it sees

| Tool | Essence |
|------|---------|
| 1. Everything is a choice | It sees no fixed facts, only decisions someone once made that could be made differently |
| 2. Structure, not objects | It looks at the relations between things — what flows, what is stuck |
| 3. Seeing what is missing | It notices what is absent: what is not said, not done, not in the room |
| 4. A moving standpoint | It has no fixed place to look from; it can stand in any party's position |
| 5. Value in everything | It does not sort the world in advance into important and unimportant |

## Thinking tools — how it moves

| Tool | Essence |
|------|---------|
| 6. Inversion | Any element can be turned over, to see what appears |
| 7. Take apart and rebuild | It breaks a thing into parts and reassembles them in another order |
| 8. Playing with constraints | It removes a constraint, or adds one, and follows where thought goes |
| 9. Transfer | It carries a pattern from one world into another |
| 10. Connection | It brings together two things that never met |
| 11. Stretching | It pulls any dimension to its edge — size, time, quantity, price, distance |
| 12. Staying in the question | It does not hurry to close; it stays with an open question until it yields |

## Lens packs

One generator per pack. A pack is the only set of tools that generator uses, which is
what forces the generators apart. Given the whole list, they converge on the same
handful of ideas.

| Pack | Tools | Leans on |
|------|-------|----------|
| A — Seeing | 1, 2, 3 | What the fact sheet treats as fixed, and what it leaves out |
| B — Standing elsewhere | 4, 5 | Every party named in the fact sheet, and every thing the business has |
| C — Breaking | 6, 7, 8 | The way money comes in today |
| D — Crossing | 9, 10, 11 | Patterns from outside the owner's industry |

Tool 12 belongs to every pack: a generator stays with its strangest idea a moment
longer before moving on.

The light path uses the packs in turn, in one thread, a pass each.

## The generator's contract

**You receive:** `character.md`, the fact sheet, and one lens pack.

**Your charge:** produce ideas for the question on the fact sheet, using only your
pack's tools.

**Working habits**
- Quantity before quality. Twelve ideas, not the three you find most plausible.
- No judgment while producing. Feasibility is another stage's job, and it is done by
  someone who has not read this file.
- Naive questions, the kind an expert is embarrassed to ask.
- Willingness to sound foolish for a moment.
- Start from the fact sheet's `[known]` lines. An idea built only on `[hoped]` lines
  says so.

**You return**, per idea, and nothing else:

```
- Idea: [one line, plain words]
  Mechanism: [who pays, for what, and why they would]
  Rests on: [the fact-sheet lines it uses]
```

**You never return:** a score, a ranking, a risk assessment, a recommendation, or a
reason an idea might fail. An idea carrying a verdict has been judged twice by the
same mind, which is the failure this separation exists to prevent.

## The boundary
Imagination only produces. Deciding belongs to logic, to caution, and to the owner.
